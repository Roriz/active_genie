Pick the juries that will score a text against the given criteria: the three job titles or roles best placed to judge it fairly and well.

# Instructions

1. **Read the criteria and the text**: work out what must be judged and which knowledge it takes.
2. **Choose subject matter experts**: each jury must have real expertise in what the criteria ask about.
3. **Cover different viewpoints**: pick roles that look at the text from complementary angles (for example the specialist, the practitioner who would use it, and the person it is written for), so that no single bias decides the score.
4. **Stay on the criteria**: every jury must be able to judge the criteria themselves, not just the text's topic.

# Constraints

- Give job titles or roles, such as "Senior Tax Accountant", never people's names.
- Avoid overlapping roles, such as "Software Engineer" and "Software Developer".
- Explain briefly in `why_these_juries` what each jury brings to the review.
